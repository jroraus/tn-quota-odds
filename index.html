Lowest priority points that drew each TWRA quota hunt, and the share drawn at each point level, from TWRA's published draw results.

How to read this

TWRA rules

  • "Each point system is unique and separate for the different quota hunts, i.e., WMA Big Game, Spring Turkey, WMA, etc." Source
  • "If you are successfully drawn, then your priority points will reset to zero." Source
  • "Applications are automatically sorted by priority point groups, from highest to lowest." Source
  • "Priority points in a party will be averaged and rounded to the nearest whole number." (WMA Big Game) Source
