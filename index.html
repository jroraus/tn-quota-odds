Tennessee Quota Hunts: points needed to draw

Minimum priority points that drew each hunt, from TWRA's published draw results.

Source: TWRA, as of Oct 20, 2026. Data comes from the draw-results tables on the WMA Big Game (2026, 2025) and Spring Turkey (2026) pages. Regions come from TWRA's regional WMA lists. Elk results were "Coming Soon" on TWRA's elk page, and no draw tables were published for waterfowl, quail, crane or Cherokee.

"Min pts" is the lowest point level with at least one winner. "Odds at min" is winners divided by applicants at that level. TWRA counts an applicant under every hunt they listed, and parties use averaged points, so treat these figures as a guide, not a guarantee. This is an unofficial site and is not affiliated with TWRA. Always confirm with TWRA.
